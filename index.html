Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about AURA programs, coaching, and subscriptions.

How does the AI Fitness Coach gather biometric data?
AURA integrates with standard smartwatch APIs (including Apple Health, Google Fit, and Garmin). Once synced, the system anonymously evaluates sleep variability, resting pulses, and daily energy outputs to dynamically adapt your workouts.
Are the diet recommendations customizable for food allergies?
Absolutely. Our culinary science backend allows you to toggle parameters like gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, keto, and common allergens. The system rebuilds the micronutrient macros automatically.
Can I switch plans or cancel at any point?
Yes, all AURA subscriptions run on a flexible month-to-month schedule. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your tier instantly from your account panel settings without any fees.
Is the Elite Plan suitable for absolute beginners?
Yes, it is highly recommended. The Elite tier provides direct human oversight alongside daily data reviews, ensuring beginners receive correct physiological advice, preventing potential injury or initial fatigue.
